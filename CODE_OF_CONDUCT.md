# Code of conduct

CanastraEngine is built by people who like Lineage II and want to make something good together. These
rules keep the community a place where that works. They apply to issues, pull requests, discussions,
chats and forums where the project is represented.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers. Everyone started somewhere.
- Critique ideas and code, never people. Explain the why behind a review comment.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Keep discussions on topic and in English or Portuguese, so others can follow and help.
- Credit the work of others, including the projects and people we learn from.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sharing or asking for leaked source code, cracked clients, or other people's private files.
- Posting anyone's personal information without their permission.
- Selling or advertising CanastraEngine itself, or forks of it, which the [license](LICENSE) forbids.
  Promoting your own extensions or servers is fine in the places set aside for it.
- Spam, repeated off-topic posts, and deliberately disrupting discussions.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, contact the maintainers privately through the repository owner's profile.
Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers decide what counts as a violation and may, depending on its seriousness:

1. ask the person to stop, privately or in public;
2. remove comments, commits or other contributions;
3. block the person temporarily;
4. block the person permanently.

Serious violations, such as threats or harassment, may skip straight to a permanent block.
